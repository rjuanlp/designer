# Community conduct

Treat contributors with respect regardless of identity, background or experience.
Critique technical decisions without personal attacks. Harassment, threats,
discriminatory language and disclosure of private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation.
For sensitive reports, contact a maintainer privately through the contact method
listed on their GitHub profile; do not disclose personal details in public issues.
Maintainers involved in a complaint should recuse themselves. Responses should be
proportionate and protect the reporter's privacy.
